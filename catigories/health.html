Understanding Health & Fitness Metrics

Established formulas such as Body Mass Index (BMI), Mifflin-St Jeor Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), and the U.S. Navy circumference method offer quick population-level baselines for nutrition and fitness tracking. Because individual body composition, muscle mass, age, and medical history vary widely, combining multiple indicators yields a clearer picture than relying on any single metric.

Frequently Asked Questions

BMI only divides total weight by height squared and does not distinguish lean muscle mass from adipose tissue. Combining BMI with our Body Fat Calculator gives extra context.
BMR estimates the calories your body needs at complete rest. The Daily Calorie Calculator multiplies your BMR by an activity factor to estimate your total daily energy expenditure (TDEE).

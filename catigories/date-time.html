Leap Years, Month Lengths & Daylight Saving Accuracy

Calendar math is notoriously error-prone when handled carelessly because months range from 28 to 31 days and daylight saving transitions create 23-hour and 25-hour local days. CalcMaster normalizes calendar inputs to midnight UTC before computing day counts and respects exact Gregorian leap-year rules (divisible by 4, excluding centuries unless divisible by 400).

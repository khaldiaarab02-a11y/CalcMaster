Making Better Financial Estimates

Whether you are comparing mortgage terms, checking take-home hourly earnings, or verifying a VAT invoice, small changes in interest rates, compounding frequency, or fee assumptions can add up substantially over time. Our financial calculators display the exact formula behind every output and list clear baseline assumptions.

What Each Financial Group Helps You Solve

  • Borrowing & Amortization: Use the Loan and Mortgage calculators to compare monthly installments and lifetime interest cost across loan terms.
  • Saving & Investing: Compare Simple Interest against Compound Interest, Savings, and Investment growth trajectories.
  • Paychecks, Pricing & Taxes: Convert wages with Salary and Hourly Pay, or compute Discounts, Sales Tax, VAT, and Profit Margin.

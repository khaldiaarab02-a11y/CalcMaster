About CalcMaster Basic & Math Calculators

Everyday math tasks rarely need heavy spreadsheet software—yet black-box calculators often hide intermediate steps. Every math utility in this category exposes its underlying formula, checks edge cases such as zero denominators or negative roots, and formats outputs clearly so you can double-check homework, budgets, measurements, or code.

Highlights of This Collection

  • Safe expression parsing: Our Scientific Calculator evaluates parentheses, operator precedence, and trigonometric or logarithmic functions with a custom shunting-yard tokenizer—never unsafe string execution.
  • Exact fraction reduction: The Fraction and Ratio calculators reduce fractions and proportions using Euclid's greatest common divisor algorithm before displaying decimals.
  • Statistical & stochastic tools: Quickly inspect center and spread with the Average Calculator or run fair integer and decimal draws in the Random Number Generator.

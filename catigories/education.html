Academic Grading & Weighted Averages

Syllabi rarely weight every assignment equally—a midterm or final exam often counts for 25% to 50% of a final mark, while college GPAs weight each letter grade by credit hours. Using a weighted calculator ensures you know exactly where your standing sits before exam week.

Metric, US Customary & Technical Conversion Standards

Whether you are checking tire pressure in PSI vs. kPa, converting oven recipes between Celsius and Fahrenheit, measuring real estate in square feet vs. acres, or comparing drive capacities in gigabytes vs. gibibytes, exact reference factors prevent costly rounding drift.

  • Exact physical constants: Length uses 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly; mass uses 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg; pressure anchors on 1 atm = 101,325 Pa.
  • One-click unit swap: Every converter includes a swap button and a live table displaying all units simultaneously.

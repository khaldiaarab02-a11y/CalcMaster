Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 2026. Transparent information on CalcMaster's current browser storage usage, inactive ad placeholders, and browser controls.

1. What Are Cookies & Browser Storage?

HTTP cookies are small text files placed on your browser by websites or embedded third-party services. Web browsers also provide client-side localStorage so pages can remember simple interface preferences directly on your device without sending them to a server on every request.

2. What CalcMaster Actually Uses Right Now

Storage Item Type Purpose
calcmaster_theme localStorage (Functional) Remembers your chosen Light or Dark appearance mode locally in your browser.
First-Party Tracking / Session Cookies None set CalcMaster requires no login accounts and sets no first-party tracking cookies.
Analytics Cookies None active No third-party behavioral analytics scripts are loaded in the current codebase.

3. Advertising Slots & Future Google AdSense Cookies

CalcMaster layouts reserve non-intrusive placeholders labeled ADVERTISEMENT SPACE for potential monetization via Google AdSense after site review. In the current build (adsense.enabled: false), no Google AdSense scripts or advertising cookies are loaded.

If Google AdSense is activated in the future, Google and its advertising partners may set cookies to deliver advertisements, limit repetitive ad impressions, detect invalid traffic, and display personalized or non-personalized ads according to your region's consent requirements. You can opt out of Google ad personalization via Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Managing or Clearing Cookies & Local Storage

You can block cookies, delete existing site data, or restrict localStorage in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) under Privacy & Security or Site Settings. Blocking localStorage will not prevent any calculator on CalcMaster from running—it only resets Light/Dark mode back to your system default on refresh.

Website & Calculation Disclaimer

Effective Date: November 2026. Important scope boundaries and mathematical assumptions for CalcMaster's online calculators and educational guides.

1. General Informational Purpose

All calculators, unit converters, comparison tables, and educational articles on CalcMaster are provided free of charge strictly for informational, reference, and educational purposes. Although each formula is implemented and tested against standard mathematical equations, results depend on user-entered inputs, rounding precision, and simplified real-world models.

2. Health & Fitness Disclaimer (Not Medical Advice)

Medical Notice: Health calculators on CalcMaster—including BMI, BMR, Daily Calorie (TDEE), Ideal Weight, Water Intake, and Body Fat estimators—generate population-level mathematical estimates only. They do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition and are not a substitute for personalized care from a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified healthcare provider.

Standard screening equations cannot account for individual muscle-to-fat composition, medical history, pregnancy, medication effects, metabolic disorders, or pediatric growth charts.

3. Financial, Tax & Business Disclaimer (Not Financial Advice)

Financial Notice: Loan, Mortgage, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Savings, Investment, Salary, Hourly Pay, Profit Margin, Discount, Sales Tax, VAT, ROI, ROAS, Break-Even, Revenue, and Markup tools are strictly for educational exploration and budgeting illustration. They do not constitute personalized financial, investment, accounting, mortgage, or tax advice.

Key modeling assumptions to keep in mind:

  • Loans & mortgages: Standard fixed-rate amortization models exclude closing costs, lender origination fees, credit-tier rate adjustments, variable-rate resets, and regional compounding differences unless explicitly entered.
  • Taxes & payroll: Sales tax, VAT, and salary tools apply flat user-supplied percentages or standard pay-period divisors and do not compute statutory income tax withholding, national insurance, or local deductions.
  • Currency display: Selecting USD, CAD, GBP, AUD, or EUR formats currency symbols only and does not apply live foreign exchange rates.

4. Date, Education & Unit Conversion Notes

  • Date & time: Working-days and age calculations follow standard Gregorian rules and UTC calendar boundaries; public holiday lists and legal age-rollover definitions vary by jurisdiction.
  • Education: GPA and grade tools assume standard 4.0 unweighted scales and user-entered weights; always confirm official grading policies with your school or registrar.
  • Unit converters: Conversions rely on internationally recognized SI, US customary, and imperial definitions and should be double-checked when used for safety-critical engineering or laboratory work.

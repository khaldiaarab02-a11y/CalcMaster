How to Calculate a Discount & Stacked Sale Percentages

Financial · 4 min read

Retail promotions frequently combine a storewide markdown with an extra coupon code at checkout. Understanding multiplicative discounts prevents surprises at the register.

Why Stacked Discounts Don't Simply Add Together

Final Price = Original × (1 − d₁) × (1 − d₂)

The second discount applies to the already-reduced price, not the original sticker price.

25% Off + Extra 10% Off on $120: First markdown leaves $120 × 0.75 = $90.00. Taking an extra 10% off $90 gives $90 × 0.90 = $81.00. Your true effective discount is 32.5%, not 35%.

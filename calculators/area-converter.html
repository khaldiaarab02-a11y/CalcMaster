Area Conversion Factors

Because area is two-dimensional, linear scale ratios are squared (1 ft² = 0.09290304 m², 1 hectare = 10,000 m², and 1 international acre = 43,560 ft² = 4,046.8564224 m²):

Converted Area = (Input Value × From Factor in m²) ÷ To Factor in m²

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Type a non-negative surface or lot area value.
  2. Select your source and destination units (e.g., Acres to Square Feet or m² to ft²).
  3. Review the headline figure and the 10-unit equivalent table underneath.

Practical Examples

1. Residential Lot Size (2 Acres to Square Feet): 2 × 43,560 equals exactly 87,120 sq ft (or 8,093.71 m² / 0.809371 hectares).

2. Agricultural Parcel (1.5 Hectares to Acres): 15,000 m² ÷ 4,046.8564224 equals 3.70658072 acres.

Frequently Asked Questions

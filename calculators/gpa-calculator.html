Standard 4.0 GPA Formula

Each course contributes quality points equal to its numeric grade point value multiplied by its credit hours. Semester and cumulative GPA divide total quality points by total credits:

Semester GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Course Credits) ÷ Σ(Course Credits)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Choose each course's expected or earned letter grade from the 4.0 plus/minus scale (e.g., A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3).
  2. Enter each course's credit weight (such as 3 or 4 credits). Use + Add Course for extra classes.
  3. Optionally supply your Prior Cumulative GPA and Prior Completed Credits to project your updated transcript GPA.

Practical Examples

1. Four-Course Term (13 Credits): Taking an A (4.0) in a 3-credit class, A- (3.7) in a 4-credit lab, B+ (3.3) in a 3-credit class, and B (3.0) in a 3-credit class totals 45.70 quality points ÷ 13 credits = 3.52 Semester GPA.

2. Updating Cumulative GPA: Combining that 45.70 / 13 term with a prior 3.40 GPA across 45 prior credits (153.00 quality points) gives 198.70 ÷ 58 = 3.43 Cumulative GPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

At most universities, Pass/Fail (Credit/No Credit) and zero-credit courses are excluded from GPA calculations, so omit them from this list.
Some institutions omit minus grades, award 4.3 for A+, or add +0.5/+1.0 weighting for AP/Honors classes. Check your registrar's official grading scale.
Academic Note: Uses the common US 4.0 unweighted plus/minus scale (A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3.0). Confirm institutional repeat-course or honors policies with your registrar.

Standard Loan Payment Formula

Fixed-rate installment loans (such as auto loans and personal loans) use the standard annuity amortization formula:

M = P × [ i(1 + i)^n ] / [ (1 + i)^n − 1 ]
  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Loan principal amount
  • i = Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total number of monthly payments (Years × 12)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your preferred currency symbol (changing currency updates formatting only and does not apply foreign exchange rates).
  2. Enter the total borrowed principal, annual interest rate (APR), and repayment term in years or months.
  3. Click Calculate to review your monthly payment, total interest, and first 12 months of amortization.

Practical Examples

1. Five-Year Auto Loan: Borrowing $25,000 at 6.5% APR for 5 years (60 months) yields a monthly rate i = 0.065 / 12 = 0.0054167. The monthly payment is $489.15, and total interest over 5 years is $4,349.22.

2. 0% Promotional Financing: Financing $3,600 over 24 months at 0% APR divides principal evenly across all periods: $3,600 / 24 = $150.00/mo with $0.00 interest.

U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula

Developed by Hodgdon and Beckett at the Naval Health Research Center, the circumference formula uses measurements in centimeters (converted from inches automatically if Imperial is selected):

Men: %BF = 495 / [1.0324 − 0.19077·log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456·log10(height)] − 450
Women: %BF = 495 / [1.29579 − 0.35004·log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100·log10(height)] − 450

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your sex (women include hip circumference in addition to neck and waist) and measurement unit (Imperial inches/lbs or Metric cm/kg).
  2. Measure circumferences snugly with a flexible tape without compressing the skin (neck below the larynx; waist horizontally at the navel for men or narrowest torso for women; hips at the widest point).
  3. Enter height, weight, and circumference values, then click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Imperial Male Example (70 in tall, 175 lbs, 15 in neck, 33 in waist): Computes 15.5% estimated body fat (ACE Fitness tier), with 27.1 lbs fat mass and 147.9 lbs lean body mass.

2. Metric Female Example (168 cm tall, 64 kg, 33 cm neck, 74 cm waist, 97 cm hip): Yields 26.6% estimated body fat (ACE Average tier), with 17.0 kg fat mass and 47.0 kg lean mass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tape-measure equations are convenient at-home screening tools typically within 3–4% of hydrostatic weighing or DEXA for average body types, whereas DEXA and BodPod directly measure tissue density. Measure consistently at the same time of day to track trends accurately.
The American Council on Exercise (ACE) groups adult body fat ranges into Essential Fat (2–5% M / 10–13% F), Athletes (6–13% M / 14–20% F), Fitness (14–17% M / 21–24% F), Average (18–24% M / 25–31% F), and Above Average (25%+ M / 32%+ F).
Medical & Health Disclaimer: Circumference-based body fat formulas provide approximate non-clinical estimates only and do not diagnose obesity, malnutrition, or any medical condition. Tape placement variations, hydration, and individual anatomy affect results. Consult a physician or sports medicine professional for clinical body composition assessments.

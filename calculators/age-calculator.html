How Chronological Age Is Calculated

Rather than dividing days by a rough 365.25 average, CalcMaster walks actual Gregorian month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) in UTC so daylight-saving time shifts never add or drop a day:

Age = (Year_2 − Year_1) yrs, (Month_2 − Month_1) mos, (Day_2 − Day_1) days  (with calendar month borrow)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  2. Leave the target date on today's date, or set a past/future target date to find your age on a milestone date.
  3. Click Calculate to review your years/months/days breakdown, total days lived, and days remaining until your next birthday.

Practical Examples

1. Leap-Year Birthdate (2000-02-29 to 2024-02-29): Across six leap years (2000, 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024 span), chronological age is exactly 24 yrs, 0 mos, 0 days, totaling 8,766 days (1,252 wks, 2 d) with 366 days until turning 25. In non-leap 2025, Feb 29 rolls to Mar 1 (366 days after Feb 29, 2024).

2. Milestone Check (1995-06-15 to 2030-01-01): Calculates 34 yrs, 6 mos, 17 days, or 414 mos, 17 d and 12,619 days, with 165 days remaining until turning 35.

Frequently Asked Questions

When computing the next birthday countdown in a non-leap year, a February 29 birthdate resolves to March 1—matching standard legal age conventions in many jurisdictions.
Both dates are normalized to UTC midnight before subtracting timestamps and dividing by 86,400,000 ms, preventing 23-hour or 25-hour daylight saving days from skewing day counts.
Calendar Note: Calculations use the standard proleptic Gregorian calendar and compare whole calendar dates without time-of-birth hours. For official age verification, always consult governing local statutes.

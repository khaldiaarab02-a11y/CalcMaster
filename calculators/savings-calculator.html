Savings Accumulation Formula

With initial savings P, recurring monthly deposit C, monthly rate r = annual rate / 12, and total months n:

Balance = P(1 + r)^n + C × [ ((1 + r)^n − 1) / r ]

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your preferred currency symbol and enter your current starting savings balance.
  2. Enter your planned monthly deposit amount and expected annual savings rate (%).
  3. Set the time period in years and enter an optional target savings goal.
  4. Click Calculate to see your projected balance, deposits vs. interest earned, and goal completion percentage.

Practical Examples

1. Five-Year Emergency & Down-Payment Fund: Starting with $2,500 and saving $400/month for 5 years (60 months) at 4.25% deposits $26,500.00 total and earns $3,278.97 in interest, reaching $29,778.97 (119.1% of a $25,000 goal).

2. Two-Year Travel / Car Fund: Starting with $1,000 and adding $250/month at 4.00% for 2 years (24 months) yields $7,318.86 ($7,000.00 deposited + $318.86 interest).

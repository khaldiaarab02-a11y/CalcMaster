BMI Formulas & Standard WHO Categories

Body Mass Index expresses weight relative to the square of height for adult screening:

Metric: BMI = kg / (m)²  |  Imperial: BMI = (703 × lbs) / (inches)²
  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obesity: 30.0 and above (Class I: 30–34.9, Class II: 35–39.9, Class III: 40+)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Choose your preferred measurement system tab: Imperial (ft/in, lbs) or Metric (cm, kg).
  2. Enter your standing height and current body weight.
  3. Click Calculate to review your BMI score, WHO category, healthy reference weight range, and BMI Prime ratio.

Practical Examples

1. Imperial Example (5 ft 9 in, 165 lbs): Converting height to 69 inches yields BMI = 24.4 (Normal weight), with a BMI Prime of 0.97 and a reference 18.5–24.9 BMI weight span of 125.3 – 168.6 lbs.

2. Metric Example (175 cm, 75 kg): Converting height to 1.75 m computes 75 / (1.75)² = 24.5 (Normal weight), with a healthy reference range of 56.7 – 76.3 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

BMI Prime divides your calculated BMI by 25.0 (the upper limit of the standard normal weight range). Values between 0.74 and 1.00 fall within the standard normal BMI band.
BMI evaluates total body weight relative to height without distinguishing lean muscle tissue from adipose body fat. Athletes and strength-trained individuals can supplement BMI with our Body Fat Calculator.
Medical & Health Disclaimer: BMI is a general population screening estimate and does not diagnose any medical condition. It does not directly measure body fat percentage, fat distribution, muscle mass, or bone density, and thresholds may differ for children, seniors, pregnant individuals, and certain ethnic groups. Consult a licensed healthcare professional for individualized medical advice.

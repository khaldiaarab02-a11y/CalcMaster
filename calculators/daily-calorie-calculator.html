TDEE & Calorie Target Formula

Total Daily Energy Expenditure scales resting BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) by an activity factor reflecting daily movement and exercise:

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor  |  Deficit/Surplus = TDEE ± Calorie Adjustment

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Switch between Imperial and Metric tabs according to your preferred units.
  2. Enter your age, sex, height, and current weight.
  3. Select the activity multiplier that best matches your typical full-week routine and click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Moderately Active Female (30 yrs, 5 ft 6 in, 145 lbs): Resting BMR of ~1,394 kcal multiplied by 1.55 produces 2,161 kcal/day maintenance, 1,911 kcal/day mild loss, and 1,661 kcal/day for a 500-kcal/day deficit.

2. Lightly Active Male (35 yrs, 180 cm, 82 kg): Resting BMR is 10(82) + 6.25(180) − 5(35) + 5 = 1,775 kcal; multiplying by 1.375 estimates 2,441 kcal/day maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our calculator applies a conservative 1,200 kcal/day lower bound for automatic deficit displays because diets below that threshold risk micronutrient insufficiency without clinical supervision.
Recalculate every 5–10 pounds (2–5 kg) of weight change or whenever your weekly exercise frequency shifts substantially.
Medical & Nutrition Disclaimer: TDEE and calorie goals are general statistical estimates for educational planning only and do not diagnose or treat any medical condition. Energy requirements vary significantly by individual health status, pregnancy/lactation, medications, and lean muscle mass. Consult a licensed physician or registered dietitian before starting any weight-loss or specialized nutrition regimen.

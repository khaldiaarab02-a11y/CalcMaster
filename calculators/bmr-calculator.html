Mifflin-St Jeor BMR Equation

Validated in clinical nutrition research, the Mifflin-St Jeor formula uses weight in kilograms, height in centimeters, and age in years:

Men: BMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × kg) + (6.25 × cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select Imperial or Metric measurement units.
  2. Enter your adult age (15–110), biological sex selection for the Mifflin-St Jeor constant, height, and current body weight.
  3. Click Calculate to view resting BMR alongside sedentary and moderately active maintenance baselines.

Practical Examples

1. Adult Male (30 yrs, 5 ft 10 in, 175 lbs): Converting to 177.8 cm and 79.38 kg gives 10(79.38) + 6.25(177.8) − 5(30) + 5 = 1,760 kcal/day resting BMR, or 2,112 kcal/day sedentary.

2. Adult Female (28 yrs, 165 cm, 62 kg): Computing 10(62) + 6.25(165) − 5(28) − 161 gives 1,350 kcal/day resting BMR and 2,093 kcal/day moderately active.

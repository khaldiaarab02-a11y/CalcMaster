Gross vs. Net Revenue Waterfall

Net realized revenue removes promotional discounts from gross bookings first, then deducts the expected refund rate from discounted billings:

Gross = Units × Price  |  After Discount = Gross × (1 − d)  |  Net Revenue = After Discount × (1 − r)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select currency and enter total Units Sold and your standard List Price per Unit.
  2. Provide the average promotional Discount % and expected Refund / Return Rate % (0 if not applicable).
  3. Click Calculate to compare gross top-line sales against net realized revenue and net yield per unit.

Practical Examples

1. Product Launch Month (500 Units @ $79, 10% Discount, 3% Refunds): Gross sales equal $39,500.00. Subtracting the 10% discount ($3,950.00) and 3% refund rate on $35,550.00 ($1,066.50) gives $5,016.50 in deductions and $34,483.50 Net Revenue ($68.97/unit).

2. Seasonal Promotion (1,200 Units @ $50, 20% Off, 5% Refunds): Gross sales of $60,000.00 drop to $48,000.00 after discounts and $45,600.00 Net Revenue after $2,400.00 in refunds ($14,400.00 total deductions).

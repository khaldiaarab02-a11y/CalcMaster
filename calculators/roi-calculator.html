ROI & Annualized ROI Formulas

Simple ROI divides net profit by initial capital outlay, while annualized ROI accounts for holding duration via compound annual growth rate (CAGR):

ROI % = ((Returned − Invested) ÷ Invested) × 100  |  Annualized = ((Returned ÷ Invested)1/Years − 1) × 100

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your preferred currency symbol and enter how many years the investment was held (use decimals such as 0.5 for 6 months).
  2. Enter your initial cost basis under Amount Invested and total ending proceeds under Final Amount Returned.
  3. Click Calculate to examine total ROI %, dollar net gain/loss, and annualized rate.

Practical Examples

1. Two-Year Capital Investment ($10,000 to $14,500): Net profit equals $4,500.00, giving a cumulative ROI of +45.00% and an annualized return of 20.42% / yr.

2. Three-Year Business Equipment Upgrade ($5,000 to $6,655): Net gain is $1,655.00 (+33.10% total ROI), matching an annualized compound return of exactly 10.00% / yr.

Day Counting & Weekday Classification

Each date in the range is inspected in UTC to count total elapsed days and classify every day as either a Monday–Friday weekday or a Saturday–Sunday weekend day:

Total Days = Weekdays + Weekend Days  |  Weeks = Total Days / 7

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Choose the Start Date and End Date of your range.
  2. Optionally check Include end date if you want both dates included in the total and weekday/weekend counts.
  3. Click Calculate to inspect total days, weekday breakdown, weekend count, and weeks.

Practical Examples

1. Spring Semester / Quarter Planning (2026-03-01 to 2026-06-30): Exclusive of June 30, there are 121 days (86 weekdays, 35 weekend days, 17.29 weeks). Including June 30 yields 122 days (87 weekdays).

2. Leap-Year February Check (2024-02-01 to 2024-03-01): Exclusive of March 1, accounts for Feb 29 and returns 29 days (21 weekdays, 8 weekend days, 4.14 weeks).

Frequently Asked Questions

This tool separates calendar days into Monday–Friday weekdays and Saturday–Sunday weekend days without subtracting holidays. If you need custom public holiday exclusions, use our Working Days Calculator.
Every year divisible by 4 (except century years not divisible by 400, such as 1900) automatically includes February 29, adding one calendar day whenever February 29 falls inside your chosen window.
Calendar Note: Day counts follow standard Gregorian calendar rules and treat full dates uniformly across all time zones. Verify statutory notice-period definitions when applying counts to legal or financial deadlines.

Hydration Calculation Formula

This estimator uses a baseline of 33 mL per kilogram of body weight plus 350 mL for every 30 minutes of exercise, scaled by your selected climate multiplier:

Liters/Day = [ (Weight_kg × 0.033) + (Exercise_min / 30) × 0.35 ] × Climate Multiplier

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your current body weight and choose Pounds (lbs) or Kilograms (kg).
  2. Enter your average daily moderate-to-vigorous exercise duration in minutes.
  3. Select your climate setting and click Calculate to view liters, fluid ounces, and 250 mL cups per day.

Practical Examples

1. Everyday Moderate Routine (160 lbs, 30 min exercise, Temperate): Converting 160 lbs to 72.57 kg yields 2.39 L baseline + 0.35 L exercise = 2.74 L/day (93 fl oz/day or 11.0 cups).

2. Hot Climate Endurance Day (70 kg, 60 min exercise, Hot/Humid +20%): Baseline 2.31 L + 0.70 L exercise = 3.01 L, scaled by 1.20 to 3.61 L/day (122 fl oz/day).

Investment Growth Formula

To match standard annualized market return conventions (R), the calculator derives the equivalent monthly growth rate r_m = (1 + R)^(1/12) − 1 and compounds both initial capital and recurring monthly deposits over N = 12 × Years months:

FV = Start(1 + r_m)^N + Contribution × [ ((1 + r_m)^N − 1) / r_m ]

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Choose your preferred currency symbol and enter your starting portfolio principal.
  2. Enter your regular monthly contribution and expected annualized rate of return (e.g., 7% or 8% for nominal equity estimates, or lower to model inflation-adjusted real returns).
  3. Enter your investment timeline in years and click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. 20-Year Retirement Index Contribution: Starting with $15,000 and contributing $500/month for 20 years at an 8% effective annual return invests $135,000.00 out of pocket, generates $219,413.89 in gains, and grows to $354,413.89 (a 2.63x multiple).

2. Pure Monthly Dollar-Cost Averaging: Starting at $0 and investing $750/month for 10 years at 6% effective annual return deposits $90,000.00 and yields $121,855.08 ($31,855.08 in gains, 1.35x multiple).

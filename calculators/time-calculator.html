Base-60 Time Arithmetic Formula

Both durations are converted to whole seconds before addition or subtraction, eliminating manual carry and borrow mistakes:

Total Seconds = (Hours × 3,600) + (Minutes × 60) + Seconds

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter whole hours, minutes, and seconds for the first duration (leave blank or enter 0 for unused units).
  2. Select Add (+) or Subtract (−).
  3. Enter the second duration and click Calculate to view normalized hours, minutes, seconds, and decimal hours.

Practical Examples

1. Adding Two Video Segments: Combining 2h 45m 30s and 1h 35m 45s carries 75 seconds into +1 minute 15 seconds and 81 minutes into +1 hour 21 minutes, producing 4h 21m 15s (4.3542 hrs, 15,675 secs).

2. Subtracting Remaining Flight Time: Subtracting 1h 50m 20s flown from a 5h 15m 0s leg yields 3h 24m 40s remaining (204.67 mins).

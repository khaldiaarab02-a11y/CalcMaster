Salary Conversion Formulas

On a standard 52-week full-time calendar (2,080 annual hours at 40 hours/week and 260 annual workdays at 5 days/week):

Annual = Hourly × Hours/Week × 52  |  Monthly = Annual / 12  |  Bi-Weekly = Annual / 26

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your currency and enter the compensation figure from your job offer or paycheck.
  2. Choose the input pay period (e.g., Per Hour or Per Year).
  3. Adjust hours per week and workdays per week to match your contract schedule, then click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Hourly to Annual Offer Comparison: A wage of $35.00/hour for 40 hours/week equals $72,800.00/year, $6,066.67/month, $2,800.00 bi-weekly, and $280.00/day.

2. Annual Salary to Hourly Equivalent: A salaried role paying $85,000/year on a 40-hour schedule translates to $40.87/hour, $3,269.23 bi-weekly, and $7,083.33/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bi-weekly pay occurs every two weeks (26 paychecks per year, dividing annual salary by 26). Semi-monthly pay occurs twice per month on fixed calendar dates (24 paychecks per year, dividing annual salary by 24).
This converter assumes 52 uniform paid weeks at your regular rate. For weekly overtime multipliers (1.5x or 2x) and fewer than 52 paid weeks, use our Hourly Pay Calculator.
Financial Assumptions & Disclaimer: All figures represent gross compensation before federal, state/provincial, or local income taxes, Social Security/Medicare/CPP/NI contributions, health premiums, or retirement deductions, assuming 52 paid weeks per year.

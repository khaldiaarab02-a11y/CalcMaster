Forward & Reverse Sales Tax Formulas

To add tax on top of a sticker price or extract pre-tax price from a tax-included total using decimal tax rate r = rate / 100:

Add Tax: Total = PreTax × (1 + r)  |  Reverse Tax: PreTax = Total / (1 + r)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your currency symbol and choose Add Sales Tax or Reverse Sales Tax.
  2. Enter the item price (or total paid receipt amount for reverse mode) and your local sales tax rate (%).
  3. Click Calculate to view the pre-tax price, tax amount, and grand total.

Practical Examples

1. Adding State & Local Tax: Adding 8.25% sales tax to a $100.00 purchase produces $100 × 0.0825 = $8.25 in tax and a final total of $108.25.

2. Reverse-Calculating a Receipt Total: If a tax-inclusive receipt totals $162.00 at an 8% tax rate, switching to Reverse mode computes $162.00 / 1.08 = $150.00 pre-tax subtotal and $12.00 sales tax.

Required Final Exam Score Formula

If your final exam represents weight w (as a decimal, e.g., 0.30 for 30%) and all completed coursework makes up (1 − w), solving for the needed exam percentage yields:

Required Exam % = (Target Grade − Current Grade × (1 − w)) ÷ w

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your current average across all completed pre-final assignments (compute it first with the Grade Calculator if unsure).
  2. Enter your desired overall course percentage (e.g., 90 for an A- or 80 for a B-).
  3. Enter how much the final exam counts toward the syllabus total (1% to 100%) and click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Stretching from 84% to 90% on a 30% Final: (90 − 84 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = (90 − 58.8) ÷ 0.30 = 104.00%, meaning an A- requires extra credit.

2. Raising an 86% to 88% on a 25% Final: (88 − 86 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = 23.5 ÷ 0.25 = 94.00% needed on the final exam.

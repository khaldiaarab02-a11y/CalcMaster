Raw Exam Score & Penalty Formula

For standard grading (zero penalty), percentage is simply (Correct ÷ Total) × 100. When negative marking is enabled, wrong answers subtract a penalty floored at zero:

Net Points = max(0, Correct − (Total − Correct) × Penalty)  |  Score % = (Net Points ÷ Total) × 100

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter the total number of exam questions or maximum points available.
  2. Enter the count of questions answered correctly (must not exceed total questions).
  3. Leave Wrong-Answer Penalty at 0 for normal tests, or enter e.g. 0.25 if each incorrect multiple-choice guess deducts a quarter point.
  4. Adjust the Passing Threshold if your course requires e.g. 70% or 75% to pass.

Practical Examples

1. Standard Quiz (43 Correct out of 50): With 0 penalty and a 60% pass mark, incorrect answers = 7, net score = 43 / 50, percentage = 86.00% (Letter Grade: B, Status: Pass).

2. Negative-Marking Entrance Exam (48 Correct of 60, 0.25 Penalty): With 12 wrong answers penalized at 0.25 each (3.00 points deducted), net score becomes 48 − 3 = 45 out of 60 = 75.00% (Letter Grade: C).

Frequently Asked Questions

Many standardized aptitude, civil-service, and university entrance exams deduct 0.25 or 0.33 points per incorrect answer to discourage random guessing.
This Exam Score Calculator grades a test you have already taken (correct vs. total questions). To calculate the score needed on an upcoming final exam, use our Final Exam Calculator.

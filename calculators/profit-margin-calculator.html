Profit Margin vs. Markup Formulas

Profit margin divides dollar profit by selling price (revenue), whereas markup divides profit by unit cost:

Profit = Revenue − Cost  |  Margin % = (Profit / Revenue) × 100  |  Markup % = (Profit / Cost) × 100

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your preferred display currency.
  2. Enter the direct unit cost or total cost of goods sold (COGS).
  3. Enter the selling price or total revenue generated, then click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Standard Retail Margin Check: If a product costs $60.00 and sells for $100.00, your profit is $40.00, gross margin is (40 / 100) × 100 = 40.00%, and cost markup is (40 / 60) × 100 = 66.67%.

2. Service Project Pricing: If a freelance deliverable incurs $150.00 in direct contractor cost and bills at $240.00, profit is $90.00, margin is 37.50%, and markup is 60.00%.

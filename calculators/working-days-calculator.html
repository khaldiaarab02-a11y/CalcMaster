How Working Days Are Computed

Starting at the selected start date, CalcMaster scans each calendar day through the end date and assigns it to one of three mutually exclusive buckets:

Working Days = Total Calendar Days − Weekend Days − Weekday Holidays

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your project or payroll Start Date and End Date.
  2. Enter any public or company holidays falling in that window in YYYY-MM-DD format, separated by commas or spaces (optional).
  3. Keep Include end date checked when counting full calendar periods (e.g., Nov 1 through Nov 30), then click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. November 2026 With Thanksgiving Holiday (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-30): Out of 30 total days, there are 9 weekend days and 1 weekday holiday (2026-11-26), leaving 20 working days.

2. Two-Week Sprint Without Holidays (2026-05-04 to 2026-05-15, Inclusive): Across 12 calendar days, 2 weekend days are excluded, resulting in exactly 10 working days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Weekend days are excluded first so a holiday falling on a weekend is not double-counted. If your organization observes a Monday/Friday substitute day off, enter that weekday's YYYY-MM-DD date instead.
Use ISO YYYY-MM-DD format (such as 2026-12-25, 2027-01-01). Any unrecognized token triggers a clear validation prompt so silent omissions never happen.
Scheduling Note: Standard workweek logic assumes Monday–Friday business days. Always verify statutory banking, court, or shipping holiday calendars for contractual SLA commitments.

Standard Reference Weight Formulas

Each equation starts with a 5-foot (60-inch) baseline weight in kilograms and adds an increment per inch above 60 inches (in_over = max(0, height_in − 60)):

Devine: 50 kg (M) / 45.5 kg (F) + 2.3 kg × in_over  |  Hamwi: 48 kg (M) / 45.5 kg (F) + 2.7 (M) / 2.2 (F) × in_over

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select Imperial (feet and inches) or Metric (centimeters).
  2. Choose your sex option for the clinical formula constants and enter your height.
  3. Click Calculate to compare the WHO healthy BMI span with Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi estimates.

Practical Examples

1. Male, 5 ft 9 in (69 in): With 9 inches over 5 feet, Devine yields 155.9 lbs, Robinson 152.3 lbs, Miller 151.9 lbs, Hamwi 159.4 lbs, and the healthy BMI range spans 125.3 lbs – 168.6 lbs.

2. Female, 165 cm (~65 in): With 4.96 inches over 60 inches, Devine gives 56.9 kg, Robinson 57.4 kg, Miller 59.8 kg, Hamwi 56.4 kg, and the healthy BMI range spans 50.4 kg – 67.8 kg.

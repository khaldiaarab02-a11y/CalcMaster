Markup vs. Gross Margin Formulas

Markup is calculated as a percentage added on top of unit cost, whereas gross margin expresses that same dollar profit as a share of the final selling price:

Selling Price = Cost × (1 + Markup % / 100)  |  Gross Margin % = (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your currency and enter the wholesale or landed Unit Cost.
  2. Enter your target Markup Percentage (e.g., 50% for 1.5x cost or 100% for keystone 2x cost).
  3. Click Calculate to review the final selling price, unit profit, and converted gross margin percentage.

Practical Examples

1. Standard 50% Markup ($40 Cost): Profit equals $40 × 50% = $20.00, giving a Selling Price of $60.00 and an equivalent Gross Margin of 33.33% ($20 ÷ $60).

2. Keystone Retail Pricing (100% Markup on $25 Cost): Profit is $25.00, Selling Price doubles to $50.00, and Gross Margin equals exactly 50.00%.

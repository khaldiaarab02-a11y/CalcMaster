Date Difference Calculation Method

Calendar differences count actual elapsed years, months, and days between dates, while total day counts compute exact elapsed 24-hour UTC intervals:

Total Days = |End_UTC − Start_UTC| / 86,400,000 ms (+ 1 day if inclusive)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pick a Start Date and an End Date.
  2. Check Include end date in count if counting both boundary days (for instance, a multi-day event inclusive of its closing day).
  3. Click Calculate to display the years/months/days duration, total days, weeks, and hours.

Practical Examples

1. Full Calendar Year (2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31): Exclusive of the final day, the span is 0 yrs, 11 mos, 30 days (364 days or 8,736 hours). Toggling inclusive adds 1 day, resulting in exactly 1 yrs, 0 mos, 0 days (365 days).

2. Multi-Year Project Lease (2024-02-15 to 2027-08-01): Computes 3 yrs, 5 mos, 17 days, spanning 1,263 days (180 wks, 3 d, accounting for leap day Feb 29, 2024).

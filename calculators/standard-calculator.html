    How the Standard Calculator Works

    A standard four-function calculator evaluates sequential arithmetic operations immediately as operators are chained, mirroring classic desktop calculators. It cleans IEEE-754 floating-point rounding artifacts (such as 0.1 + 0.2) and actively guards against division by zero and negative square roots.

    Percent Key (%): b% of a = (a × b) / 100  |  Reciprocal: 1/x (for x ≠ 0)

    Step-by-Step Instructions

    1. Enter your first number by clicking the keypad buttons or typing directly on your keyboard.
    2. Choose an arithmetic operator (+, −, ×, ÷) or single-value function (x², √x, 1/x).
    3. Enter the second number and press = (or Enter) to view the result and log it in the history strip.
    4. Use M+, M-, MR, and MC to store and reuse intermediate totals across multiple calculations.

    Practical Examples

    1. Grocery Discount Check: Suppose an item costs $85.00 and is 15% off. Enter 85 − 15 % =. Pressing % computes 15% of 85 (12.75), and pressing = returns 72.25.

    2. Memory Totaling Across Invoices: Calculate 24 × 12 = (288), press M+, then calculate 18 × 5 = (90), press M+, and press MR to recall the combined total of 378.

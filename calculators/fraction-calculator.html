Fraction Arithmetic Formulas

Fractions are converted to improper form if mixed numbers are entered, evaluated using exact integer cross-products, and reduced via the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD).

Addition: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd  |  Division: (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a × d) / (b × c)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Leave the Whole box at 0 for simple fractions, or enter a whole integer for mixed numbers.
  2. Enter whole-number numerators and non-zero denominators for both fractions and pick your arithmetic operator.
  3. Click Calculate to view the reduced fraction, mixed number, and decimal value.

Practical Examples

1. Adding 3/4 + 2/5: Common denominator is 20. Rewriting gives 15/20 + 8/20 = 23/20, which equals mixed number 1 3/20 and decimal 1.15.

2. Scaling a Baking Recipe (1 1/2 × 2/3): Convert 1 1/2 to 3/2, then multiply (3/2) × (2/3) = 6/6, simplifying to exact whole cup 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

Division by zero is mathematically undefined, so every fraction denominator must be a non-zero integer.
Entering a negative whole number (such as -2 with 1/3) applies the negative sign to the entire mixed fraction (-7/3).
Calculation Notice: Fraction reductions use exact integer Euclidean GCD simplification; decimal equivalents are rounded to at most 6 decimal places for repeating fractions such as 1/3 ≈ 0.333333.

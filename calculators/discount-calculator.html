Discount & Stacked Coupon Formula

Sequential retail markdowns multiply remaining proportions rather than adding percentages directly:

Sale Price = Original × (1 − d1/100) × (1 − d2/100)
Savings = Original − Sale Price  |  Total = Sale Price × (1 + Tax/100)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your currency and enter the item's original list price.
  2. Enter the primary discount percentage (and an optional extra coupon discount if stacking promotions, or leave as 0).
  3. Optionally enter your local sales tax rate to calculate the final register total, then click Calculate.

Practical Examples

1. Stacked Retail Coupon: On a $120.00 coat marked 25% off with an extra 10% off promo code, the first discount lowers the price to $90.00, and the second takes 10% off $90 ($9) for a final sale price of $81.00—saving $39.00 (a 32.50% combined discount, not 35%).

2. Single Discount With Sales Tax: An $80.00 item at 30% off (0% extra) drops to $56.00 (saving $24.00). With 8% sales tax added at checkout ($4.48), the final total is $60.48.

Length Conversion Methodology

Every distance unit is converted via the SI meter using exact international definitions (1 inch = 0.0254 m, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 mile = 1,609.344 m, 1 nautical mile = 1,852 m):

Converted Value = (Input Value × From Factor in Meters) ÷ To Factor in Meters

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter a non-negative measurement in Length Value.
  2. Select your source distance unit under From Unit and destination unit under To Unit.
  3. Click Swap Units anytime to reverse the conversion direction, and inspect the comparison table below to see all nine units simultaneously.

Practical Examples

1. 10K Race Distance (10 km to Miles): 10 km × 1,000 ÷ 1,609.344 equals 6.21371192 miles (10,000 meters or 10,936.13 yards).

2. Room Trim Measurement (12 in to Centimeters): Converting 12 inches yields 30.48 cm (or 1 ft / 0.3048 m).

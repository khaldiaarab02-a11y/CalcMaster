ROAS, ACoS & Break-Even Formulas

ROAS measures gross top-line revenue generated for every dollar spent on ads, whereas Break-Even ROAS reveals the minimum multiple required just to cover cost of goods:

ROAS = Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend  |  ACoS % = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue) × 100  |  Break-Even ROAS = 100% ÷ Margin %

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your Total Ad Spend and the gross Attributed Ad Revenue generated by those ads.
  2. Enter your store's Product Gross Margin % (after COGS, shipping, and payment fees) to compute your break-even ROAS floor.
  3. Click Calculate to compare your campaign's actual ROAS against break-even ROAS.

Practical Examples

1. Profitable Paid Search Campaign ($2,500 Spend → $11,250 Revenue, 50% Margin): Generates 4.50x (4.50:1) ROAS (450%) and 22.22% ACoS. Because Break-Even ROAS at 50% margin is 2.00x, this campaign is solidly profitable on variable margin.

2. Tight-Margin Campaign ($4,000 Spend → $10,000 Revenue, 30% Margin): Produces 2.50x ROAS (250%, ACoS 40.00%), which falls below the 3.33x Break-Even ROAS needed at a 30% margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

ROAS compares gross ad revenue strictly to media ad spend, ignoring product cost (COGS), agency fees, and overhead. ROI measures net profit after subtracting all costs.
ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) is the exact mathematical inverse of ROAS: a 4.00x ROAS equals a 25.00% ACoS (1 / 4 = 0.25).
Marketing Attribution Note: ROAS figures rely on platform-attributed revenue before returns, agency retainers, or fixed overhead. Compare against your Break-Even ROAS using fully loaded contribution margins.

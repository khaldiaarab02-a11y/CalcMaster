Weighted Average Formula

A weighted average multiplies each value by its importance or quantity weight before dividing by the sum of weights:

Weighted Average = Σ(Value_i × Weight_i) ÷ Σ(Weight_i)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter numeric observations under Data Value and their non-negative weights under Weight.
  2. Click + Add Row if you need more than three data pairs.
  3. Click Calculate to compare the exact weighted mean alongside the unweighted simple mean.

Practical Examples

1. Academic Rubric (Values 85, 90, 78 with Weights 2, 3, 1): Total weighted sum is 170 + 270 + 78 = 518 across weight sum 6, giving a weighted average of 86.3333 versus an unweighted mean of 84.3333.

2. Weighted Vendor Scorecard (95×5, 80×3, 70×2): Weighted sum is 475 + 240 + 140 = 855 across 10 total weight, yielding 85.5 (compared to simple mean 81.6667).

Pressure Unit Relationships

All eight pressure scales convert via the SI Pascal (1 Pa = 1 N/m², 1 bar = 100,000 Pa, 1 atm = 101,325 Pa, and 1 psi = 6,894.757293 Pa):

Converted Pressure = (Input Value × From Factor in Pa) ÷ To Factor in Pa

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter a non-negative pressure value.
  2. Choose the measurement unit shown on your gauge (e.g., psi, bar, kPa, or mmHg) and the desired output unit.
  3. Review both the converted headline result and the eight-scale comparison table below.

Practical Examples

1. Car Tire Inflation (32 psi to kPa & Bar): A tire recommended at 32 psi converts to 220.63223338 kPa or 2.206322 bar.

2. Standard Sea-Level Air Pressure (1 atm to Bar & hPa): 1 standard atmosphere equals 1.01325 bar, 1,013.25 hPa/mbar, and 101.325 kPa.

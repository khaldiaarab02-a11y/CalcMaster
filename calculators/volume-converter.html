Liquid & Cubic Volume Factors

All 11 units convert cleanly via the liter (1 L = 1,000 mL = 0.001 m³). A US liquid gallon is defined as exactly 3.785411784 L, while an Imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 L:

Converted Volume = (Input Value × From Factor in Liters) ÷ To Factor in Liters

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter the liquid or cubic volume amount.
  2. Pick the starting unit (kitchen, US liquid, imperial, or metric) and target unit.

Practical Examples

1. Fuel Container (5 US Gallons to Liters): 5 × 3.785411784 equals 18.92705892 liters (or 4.163371 Imperial gallons).

2. Recipe Measurement (2 US Cups to Milliliters): 2 × 236.5882365 equals 473.176473 mL (1 US pint / 16 US fl oz).

Percentage Formulas Explained

A percentage expresses a dimensionless ratio out of 100 ("per hundred"). Depending on what you are solving for, one of three formulas applies:

X% of Y = (X / 100) × Y  |  Part % = (X / Y) × 100  |  % Change = ((New − Initial) / |Initial|) × 100

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Pick the calculation tab matching your question: X% of Y, X is what % of Y, or % Change.
  2. Enter your two numbers in the labeled fields (decimals and negative numbers are supported where mathematically valid).
  3. Click Calculate to see the answer and exact step-by-step equation, or Reset to restore sample inputs.

Practical Examples

1. Tip Calculation (X% of Y): Leaving an 18% tip on a $65 restaurant bill gives (18 / 100) × 65 = $11.70.

2. Exam Score (X is what % of Y): Scoring 45 out of 60 points equals (45 / 60) × 100 = 75%.

3. Price Increase (% Change): A subscription rising from $12 to $15 is a ((15 − 12) / 12) × 100 = +25% increase.

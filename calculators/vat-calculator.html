VAT & GST Calculation Formulas

Let r = rate / 100. Depending on whether your starting figure excludes or includes VAT:

Add VAT: Gross = Net × (1 + r)  |  Remove VAT: Net = Gross / (1 + r)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your invoice currency (e.g., GBP, EUR, AUD, CAD, USD) and choose whether to Add VAT or Remove VAT.
  2. Enter the amount and enter the applicable VAT or GST rate (common examples: 20% UK standard VAT, 10% Australian GST, 5% Canadian GST).
  3. Click Calculate to view net value, tax portion, and gross total.

Practical Examples

1. Adding 20% UK VAT to a Net Invoice: Adding 20% VAT to a net fee of £250.00 produces £50.00 VAT and a gross total of £300.00.

2. Removing 10% Australian GST from a Gross Receipt: Removing 10% GST from a gross receipt of A$220.00 divides by 1.10, revealing a net price of A$200.00 and A$20.00 GST.

Frequently Asked Questions

Standard rates vary by jurisdiction and product type (for example, UK standard VAT is 20% with 5% reduced and 0% zero-rated items, Australian GST is 10%, and Canadian federal GST is 5% alongside provincial PST/HST). Always check current rates for your goods.
Value Added Tax is collected at each stage of production and consumer prices are commonly displayed VAT-inclusive, whereas US sales tax is charged once at final retail checkout on top of shelf prices.
Financial Assumptions & Disclaimer: This calculator applies standard single-rate net/gross VAT arithmetic without invoice line-item rounding rules or cross-border reverse-charge logic. It is provided for informational estimating only and is not tax or accounting advice.

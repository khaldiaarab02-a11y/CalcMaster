Weight & Mass Conversion Formula

All units normalize through the SI kilogram using the exact international avoirdupois pound definition (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg):

Converted Weight = (Input Value × From Factor in kg) ÷ To Factor in kg

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter a non-negative weight value in the input box.
  2. Select your source unit (e.g., kilograms, pounds, stone, or ounces) and desired target unit.
  3. Read the top converted value or consult the full reference table to compare all eight mass units at once.

Practical Examples

1. Body Weight Metric to Imperial (75 kg to Pounds): 75 ÷ 0.45359237 equals 165.34669664 lb (or 11.810478 stone).

2. Kitchen Baking Conversion (16 oz to Grams): Converting 16 ounces (1 pound) equals exactly 453.59237 g (0.453592 kg).

Frequently Asked Questions

One stone equals exactly 14 international pounds, or 6.35029318 kilograms.
A metric ton (tonne, t) equals 1,000 kg (about 2,204.62 lb), while a US short ton equals 2,000 lb (907.18474 kg).
Measurement Note: Uses standard avoirdupois ounces and pounds (not troy ounces used for precious metals). Always confirm specialized shipping or laboratory scale tolerances separately.

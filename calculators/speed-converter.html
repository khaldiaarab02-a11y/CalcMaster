Speed Conversion Factors

Each unit converts through meters per second (1 mph = 0.44704 m/s, 1 km/h = 1/3.6 m/s, 1 knot = 1,852/3,600 m/s, and standard sea-level Mach 1 = 340.29 m/s):

Converted Speed = (Input Value × From Factor in m/s) ÷ To Factor in m/s

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter a non-negative speed value.
  2. Pick the starting unit and destination unit (e.g., mph to km/h or kn to mph).
  3. Use Swap Units for quick return checks and view all six speed scales below.

Practical Examples

1. Highway Speed (60 mph to km/h): 60 × 1.609344 equals 96.56064 km/h, or exactly 88 feet/second (26.8224 m/s).

2. Wind / Marine Speed (30 Knots to mph): 30 knots equals 34.52338315 mph (55.56 km/h).

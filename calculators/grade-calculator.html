Weighted Course Grade Formula

Even if your syllabus weights have not reached 100% yet, dividing the sum of weighted scores by completed weight shows your exact running standing:

Weighted Grade % = Σ(Score % × Category Weight %) ÷ Σ(Completed Weights %)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your percentage score (from 0 to 200 if extra credit applies) for each completed assignment or category.
  2. Enter that category's percentage weight from your course syllabus.
  3. Click + Add Graded Item for additional components and press Calculate to see your running average and letter grade.

Practical Examples

1. Mid-Semester Standing (80% Completed): Scoring 88% (25% weight), 92% (25% weight), and 84% (30% weight) sums to 7,020 ÷ 80 = 87.75% (B+), with 20% of course weight left.

2. Full 100% Course Completion: Adding a 94% score worth the remaining 20% weight updates the total to (7,020 + 1,880) ÷ 100 = 89.00% (B+, 100.0% weight).

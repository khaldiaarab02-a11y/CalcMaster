Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026. This policy describes accurately how CalcMaster handles browser inputs, local storage, hosting logs, analytics, and advertising technologies.

1. Calculator Inputs Stay in Your Browser

All 50 calculators and unit converters on CalcMaster run completely client-side inside your web browser using JavaScript. Numbers, dollar amounts, loan durations, dates, grades, and health measurements that you enter are processed solely in your device's temporary memory to display results. CalcMaster does not transmit, log, sell, or store your calculator inputs in a server database.

2. Browser Preference Storage (localStorage)

CalcMaster does not require user accounts, logins, or profiles. The only persistent browser storage currently written by CalcMaster scripts is a single localStorage entry under the key calcmaster_theme, which records whether you selected "light" or "dark" mode so your appearance preference remains active across pages. You can clear this preference at any time through your browser settings.

3. Contact Form Status

In the current static release of CalcMaster, the form on the Contact Us page validates inputs strictly inside your browser and does not transmit data to an email server or database. If a server-side contact handler is enabled by the site operator in a future deployment, submitted contact fields (such as name, email address, and message) will be used solely to review and respond to your inquiry.

4. Analytics Technologies

CalcMaster currently operates without any third-party behavioral analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, session replay scripts, or third-party visitor profiling tags are loaded by the website code). If an analytics tool is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated beforehand to identify the provider, collected metrics, and opt-out controls.

5. Advertising Containers & Google AdSense

CalcMaster includes clearly marked advertisement container placeholders (ADVERTISEMENT SPACE) reserved for future Google AdSense integration. In the current configuration (adsense.enabled: false in assets/js/config.js), no Google AdSense scripts, ad networks, or advertising cookies are loaded on page views.

If Google AdSense is enabled on the published site following approval, Google and its certified partners may use cookies or local storage to serve ads, cap ad frequency, measure ad performance, and—where permitted by your regional consent preferences—display personalized advertisements. Visitors may manage ad personalization through Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Please review our Cookie Policy for details.

6. Web Hosting Server Logs

When your browser requests HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or image files from our web hosting provider, standard HTTP server infrastructure may automatically record routine connection metadata—such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, HTTP status code, and timestamp—for uptime monitoring, abuse prevention, and security diagnostics.

7. Children's Privacy

CalcMaster is a general-audience educational and utility site that does not knowingly collect personal registration data from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy whenever site functionality, hosting infrastructure, analytics, or advertising integrations change. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest update.
